Data, limits and AI use

Everything AgriChat says comes from the public datasets below, a fixed set of Filipino reply templates and a small set of Department of Agriculture guides.

NASA data and what each does

NASA POWER

Daily rain, temperature, humidity, wind, sunshine and root-zone soil wetness for the Batac grid cell, 1981 to 2025, plus 2026 to date. It drives the water budget behind every comparison and the hindcast, because it covers all 45 dry seasons from one source.

NASA SMAP Level 4

A 9 km model-assimilated estimate of root-zone soil moisture, 3-hourly, from 2015. It gives the "conditions now" soil-wetness figure, ranked against the same dates in other years. If SMAP data is more than 7 days old, soil wetness falls back to POWER and the row says which source was used.

Other data and terms

  • NOAA Oceanic Niño Index (ONI): labels each dry season as El Niño or not, using a fixed rule. NOAA CPC data; the file states no use terms and terms are not verified.
  • FAO-56 crop tables: crop water coefficients and growth stages. Used as reference values; FAO's copyright statement requires permission for reuse beyond citation.
  • Department of Agriculture guides: PinoyRice, HVCDP and BSWM documents behind the WHY answers. No reuse terms are stated on those sites; each answer cites its source tag.
  • Batac barangay directory: public municipal list, hand-transcribed.

Per-file sources, access times and terms are in the repository's provenance file.

Limits

  • Advice is at municipality scale, about 50 km, not per plot. The POWER cell averages 264 m elevation; Batac rice land is lower and slightly warmer.
  • Canal and pump supply is not visible to satellites. The water budget is rain-only and valid for rainfed fields.
  • Prices, pests and soil nutrients are outside the data.
  • The pilot covers one municipality and one rotation set.
  • Crop water figures are FAO reference values, not local field measurements.
  • FAO Kc values are designed for Penman-Monteith ET0; AgriChat uses Hargreaves.
  • Effective rain comes from FAO Training Manual 3, Table 6.
  • There is no soil filter; root-zone water capacity is one assumed number from the reviewer.
  • SMAP Level 4 is a 9 km estimate; it does not see canal irrigation or a single field. Its record starts in 2015, so the "conditions now" baseline is about 11 years with few El Niño dry seasons, and the hindcast uses POWER soil wetness instead.
  • Mungbean and soybean have few usable guide passages, and the soybean guide is in Cebuano.
  • The ONI is the ERSST v6 version from NOAA CPC; the 2025 season label is provisional.

How AI is used

  • A language model reads a farmer's free text into a command only when the keyword parser fails. It does not write advice.
  • Every advice reply is built from a fixed template and engine reason codes, so it can be traced and tested. The service works with the model switched off.
  • A model-written rewording, if ever enabled, is sent only when its numbers and crop name match the engine output and it passes the length and character checks.
  • The model never sees phone numbers. Questions about fertilizer, chemicals or pests are referred to the municipal agriculture office.
  • Phone numbers are stored only as keyed hashes; STOP deletes a farmer's profile and messages.
