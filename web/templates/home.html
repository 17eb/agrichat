Which crop after rice? Ask by SMS.

AgriChat texts rainfed rice farmers in Batac, Ilocos Norte a plain-language comparison of rice, mungbean and soybean for the next dry season. It counts how often past dry seasons had enough water, using 45 years of NASA POWER data and the El Niño record, and works on any keypad phone.

Replies are in Filipino and fit one 150-character message. Try the exchange below; nothing you type here is tied to a phone number.
